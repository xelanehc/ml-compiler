# Time per operation

GPT-2 small compiled by mlc, autotuned schedules. Each kernel's best time over several runs,
summed by kind of operation, in milliseconds, with the share of the total in parentheses.
`gemm` is the 48 dense layers, `matmul` the 24 attention products and the output projection.
The last row is the whole call, timed separately. Measured on AC power.
